# New Hire Onboarding SOP

## First day
On a new hire's first day, their manager sends a welcome message by 9:00am, IT provisions accounts (email, Slack, project tool) before lunch, and the new hire completes the security-training module. A buddy is assigned to answer questions for the first two weeks.

## First week
The new hire meets each team lead, reads the team handbook, and ships one small "starter" task by end of week to get through the full workflow once.

## Equipment
Laptops are ordered by the office manager at least 5 business days before the start date. Remote hires receive a shipping confirmation with a tracking number.
