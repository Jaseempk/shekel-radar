# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within **30 days** of purchase. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Subscriptions cancelled within the first 14 days are refunded in full. After 14 days, the current billing period is non-refundable but the subscription will not renew.

Refunds are not available for one-time setup or onboarding fees once the work has started.
