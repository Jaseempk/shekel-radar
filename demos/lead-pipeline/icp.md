# Ideal Customer Profile

Edit this to describe the prospect's best-fit customer. The scorer ranks each lead against it.

- **Who they sell to:** B2B companies, 10–200 employees.
- **Best-fit industries:** SaaS, professional services, agencies.
- **Region:** North America and Western Europe.
- **Strong signals (raise the score):** actively hiring sales roles, recently raised funding, has an outbound/SDR motion, uses a CRM.
- **Weak signals (lower the score):** under 10 employees, no clear B2B motion, agency/consultancy reselling to tiny local clients, no web presence.
- **Disqualifiers (score near 0):** B2C only, non-commercial (gov/edu/nonprofit), no website.
